## Desk adaptations

The chair side bend and chair twist use the upper-body motion from eQHmKJh20_c at 8:03 and 9:33 respectively. Replace the crossed legs with feet on the floor. The seven-second setup, 30-second side-bend sequence, and linked twist sequence are studio timings. The twist includes one second to lift the hands and five seconds holding the turn. For chair setup, see the NHS sitting exercises and Health and Wellbeing Queensland seated side stretch. Every selectable variation now has a movement diagram and a technique reference.

## Catalog animations and neck studies

Each variation has an individual movement key. For stretches in the two films, use the timestamps above and in the featured transcripts. Supported lunge (4:19), forearm pancake (1:34), chair figure four (2:58), pigeon (3:25) and the belt grip variations (5:33–6:03) use QaKuVOhikaY. The elbows-down lat variation uses eQHmKJh20_c at 7:31. The app's timed entries and releases are adaptations; the edited clips do not provide continuous timing for each variation.

For movements outside the films, the catalog links the clinical or coaching reference for each pose. These diagrams are hand-authored illustrations. Neck rotation uses seated, supine and all-fours setups. Shoulders stay quiet and direction markers show rotation on the featureless figure. Side flexion, forward flexion and the turn-then-look-down stretch are separate movements. The nine-second neck slots allocate two seconds to enter, five to pause and two to return. Diagonal rotation and flexion use a twelve-second slot. Other new thirty-second blocks ease in over three seconds and release over three; the ten-second seated chest opener uses a shorter two-and-a-half-second entry and two-second release.

## Goal practices
Front and middle splits are preparation sessions. North West Gymnastics Association leg-flexibility diagrams inform the movement families (https://nwga.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Leg-Flexibility-1-1.pdf). The studio adapts them to blocks and a counter. Each new drill uses 3 seconds to enter, 18 seconds at comfortable range, then 3 seconds to return. The daily film shows elephant walks at 2:15 and lunges at 0:28. The five-stretch film shows raised-seat and floor pancake levels at 1:21–1:49 and mentions middle splits at 1:53. The split sessions and their timing are studio arrangements.
